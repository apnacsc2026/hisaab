CSC Hisaab: Privacy Policy

CSC Hisaab is a personal bookkeeping web app (income, expenses and customer ledger) used by the shop owner.

What the app accesses

If you choose "Connect Google Drive", the app asks for the drive.file permission only. With it the app can see and edit only the files it creates itself in your own Google Drive: CSC-Hisaab-Data.json and daily backup files named CSC-Backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

Your accounting data is stored only in your own Google Drive and, as a temporary offline copy, in your own browser. We do not run any server, we do not receive, copy, sell or share your data, and the app uses no analytics or advertising.

Your control

You can disconnect from the app at any time ("Google se alag karein") or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the files from your Drive whenever you want.

Hindi saar

Aapka hisaab sirf aapke apne Google Drive me rehta hai. App sirf apni banayi hui file dekh sakti hai. Hum aapka data na dekhte hain, na kisi ko dete hain.
